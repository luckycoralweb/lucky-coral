Our Story

Our Story

Founded in Hong Kong in 1995, we began by supplying candle products to the U.S. market before expanding into ceramic homeware and retail programs for Europe and international brands.

Over the years, we have helped develop long-term manufacturing partnerships in Guangdong focused on export quality, retail standards, and product consistency.

Today, we continue supporting lifestyle, giftware, and home fragrance collections for international markets through reliable production and development experience.
